Value Pools

Two early value pools make the business case tangible.

The POC should prove value in areas NCBA Loop can measure quickly: contact-centre capacity released, and better qualification of digital customer demand.

1. Contact-centre capacity

Lua can handle repetitive, rules-based and first-line queries, while routing the conversations that need human attention with context already attached.

  • Common product questions and service guidance
  • Status checks, simple routing and structured complaint capture
  • Escalations passed to humans with context already attached

2. Digital demand qualification

Digital interest becomes more valuable when it is captured instantly and routed with clean context.

  • WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, web and campaign traffic
  • Consistent qualification questions and next-best routing
  • Less leakage from delayed follow-up, after-hours inquiries or missed handoffs

The economics should be validated during the audit using NCBA Loop’s real inquiry volumes, query mix, salary bands, conversion baseline, and channel distribution.
